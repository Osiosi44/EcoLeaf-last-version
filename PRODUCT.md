# EcoLeaf product context

## Platform and stack
Web application served by PHP with MySQLi and a MySQL-compatible database. Docker Compose is the local development environment; InfinityFree hosts the public demo at https://ecoleaf.wuaze.com/. Retain compatibility with shared PHP hosting and existing records.

## Users and jobs
Customers browse laundry services, choose item quantities, schedule a pickup, and manage orders and account details. Administrators process orders and maintain the service catalog and administrator accounts.

## Confirmed commitments
The user explicitly chose: "Keep EcoLeaf’s green branding with a modern, polished design." Preserve the existing leaf logo, service names, catalog prices, accounts, and order history. The project is a demonstration, not a payment-processing or dispatch system. Prices currently use dollars; changing currency or inventing delivery charges is outside this upgrade.

## Content and evidence
Use existing repository imagery without presenting it as proof of real facilities or staff. Reviews and dashboard figures must come from the database. Do not invent certifications, turnaround guarantees, discounts, customer counts, opening hours, or business contact details.

## Open decisions
Real business contact information, production operating policies, payments, email verification and recovery infrastructure have not been supplied.
